FAQ

White-label agency reporting: questions

Do my clients see your name anywhere?

No. The report carries your logo, colours and sender details.

How is this different from AgencyAnalytics or DashThis?

Those are dashboards you log in to, build and read. This is the finished weekly email with the commentary already written. Many agencies use both.

What access do you need?

Viewer access to each client's GA4 property and Search Console property. Nothing more.

What if tracking breaks on a client site?

The report is held and you get an alert instead of the client receiving a report full of zeros.

Can you add Google Ads, Meta Ads or Shopify?

Yes. Google Ads or Meta Ads are included in the 12-client setup. Others are quoted separately.

Is client data handled under GDPR?

GA4 and Search Console data is aggregated, not personal. I sign a data processing agreement on request and keep access read-only.
