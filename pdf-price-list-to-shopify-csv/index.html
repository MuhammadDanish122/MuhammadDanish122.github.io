FAQ

PDF to CSV conversion: questions

Can you convert scanned PDF price lists?

Yes, with an OCR step first. Scans produce more reading errors, so those rows get a manual check and anything uncertain is marked in the review sheet.

Do you upload the products to my Shopify store?

The file is ready to import, and Shopify rows come set as drafts so nothing goes live until you publish. I can run the import for you if you give me staff access.

What happens to rows that fail a check?

They go to a review sheet with the reason next to each one. Nothing is silently fixed or dropped.

Can you handle variants like size and colour?

Yes, in the Batch + repeat tier. Variant rows are grouped under one product handle.

What do you do with my supplier files?

They are used for your job only and deleted after delivery unless you want repeat runs. I am happy to sign an NDA.

Is this GDPR friendly?

Price lists rarely contain personal data. If yours do, I process them only for your job and delete them afterwards.
